Photos, three ways

The Photos page today lists every round, even the empty ones, with thumbnails in dashed boxes and the names squeezed underneath. Each version below keeps the golf and the rest of the trip apart and lets anyone add photos from the trip at any time. The phones are live: tap a photo, and tap the camera.

The pictures are the hotel’s, standing in for the group’s own. Nothing here is in the app yet.

In all three

  • Course photos stay with the golf: by round, each tagged with its hole, from the gold camera on the hole as now.
  • Trip photos get their own place: by day, for the resort, dinners, the beach, the pier.
  • A camera on the Photos page itself: take one or pick one, and say which album it goes in.
  • Tap a photo for it full screen with who took it, and swipe through.
  • Empty rounds and days stay out of the way instead of filling the page with boxes.
  • Photos still wait on the phone with no signal, and go up by themselves.
A

Two Albums

One switch at the top: On the course or The trip.

  • The course album goes by round, each photo marked with its hole.
  • The trip album goes by day.
  • The camera button adds to whichever album is open.

The simplest. Nobody has to think about where a photo goes.

B

The Magazine

A photo of the day across the top, then two rows to swipe: the course, and the trip.

  • Photo of the day is the one with the most hearts; anyone can heart a photo.
  • Each row opens into its full album.
  • Two big buttons: Add from the course and Add from the trip.

The most fun to open. Good for the evenings and the recap.

C

The Journal

The week, day by day, with the golf and the trip as two marked lanes in each day.

  • Green lane: the round that day, by hole. Gold lane: everything else.
  • Chips at the top show All, just the course, or just the trip.
  • The camera asks once: from the round, or from the trip?

Tells the story of the week. Best as a keepsake afterwards.
