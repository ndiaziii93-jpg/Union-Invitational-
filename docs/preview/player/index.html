The player card, round three

The front of 1 with the rays of 3 behind him. The last six turned into buttons: tap one and it shows what that round was worth off the handicap. The back of 1, with the web on card 2’s green, no numbers printed round it — tap a point and it tells you — and the match-ups where the legend used to be.

Still derived, not invented. Six pretend rounds at Leatherhead; every figure is worked out from them by the book’s own functions. Thirty-six points is playing to your handicap, so the ± on a tapped box is simply that round against the handicap it was played off: 38 points is +2, 30 is −6, 36 is level.
