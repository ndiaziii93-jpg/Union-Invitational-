A shot tracker for the refs: three ways

Round 2, Olympos, the par-5 3rd, with Group 1 on the tee: Duncan W, DP, Matt D and Devin O. Each phone below is the ref’s Score Entry with a counter added. Tap through them: count everybody’s shots as they’re hit, then card the hole.

In all three, the count becomes that golfer’s score on the card when the ref cards the hole, so the ref never has to add anything up. Until then the count is kept on the ref’s phone only. It survives the phone locking and needs no signal.

AA clicker on each row+ Shot beside every name

The screen the refs already use, with a + Shot button and a − on each golfer’s row. The number in the middle counts up as they play.

  • Nothing new to learn: same rows, same order, one new button.
  • All four golfers in view, so it’s easy to see who hasn’t been counted.
  • At the triple-bogey cap the row offers the pick-up.
Best forRefs who like the current screen and just want the adding-up done for them.
BFour big buttonsThe whole golfer is the button

A Count mode: the group as four big tiles. Tap anywhere on a golfer’s tile for each shot; In the hole finishes them. Once all four are in, card the hole.

  • The biggest targets: works one-thumbed, walking, in the sun.
  • Holed golfers go dark, so it’s obvious who’s still playing.
  • A small ↺ undoes a miscount.
Best forRefs who are also playing and want to tap without looking.
CShot by shotEach shot tagged as it’s hit

Each golfer gets a row of shot types: Tee, Long, Chip, Putt, Penalty. Each tap adds that shot to their trail. The total is the count; putts and penalties are counted too.

  • Three putts, water and penalty strokes mark themselves, so the recap gets them.
  • Putts per hole and per round, for free.
  • The most tapping: one choice per shot, not just one tap.
Best forA ref who enjoys it, and a recap with more to say.

My pick: B, with A’s row as the fallback

  • The big tiles are the fastest and hardest to get wrong out on the course: every golfer is one tap, and nobody gets forgotten.
  • The normal score tiles and keypad stay, so a ref who forgot to count can still type the number in.
  • C gives the recap more to work with, but it asks for a choice on every shot. That’s a job nobody keeps up past the turn.

The same in all three

  • The count fills the score only when the ref cards the hole, and the ref can still change it on the keypad.
  • Kept on the ref’s phone until then, so no signal and no battery cost. A phone that locks mid-hole comes back to the same count.
  • At par + 3 it offers the pick-up, the same prompt as now.
  • A mulligan isn’t a shot: tap − (or ↺) for the one that didn’t count.
  • Refs only. Golfers scoring their own round keep the keypad they have.
