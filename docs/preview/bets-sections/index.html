Bets: three ways to find your way

The bet cards stay exactly as they are now: the chance, Yes and No, who’s on it. What changes is how you get to them. Each phone below works: tap the tabs, the chips and the rounds. The day shown is Friday, Round 2, live, with the trip’s real teams.

The problem today: one long list, and “who finds the water?” shows up three times, once per round, with only “Round 1:” or “Round 2:” at the front to tell them apart.

ARound tabsDraftKings and FanDuel event tabs

A row of tabs pinned to the top: The week, Round 1, Round 2, Round 3, Off the course, Ours. It opens on today’s round.

  • You always know where you are: the tab stays lit as you scroll, and a plate says which round, which course, and whether it’s live.
  • The same bet never appears twice on a page. “Round 2:” disappears from every title, because the tab already says it.
  • Finished rounds keep their settled bets, so Round 1 is a record of who called it.
Best forA trip that runs day by day. “What can I bet on today?” is one tap.
BShelvesFanDuel home, pinned headers

One page, but cut into shelves with dark headers that pin to the top as you scroll: Today · Round 2, The week, Off the course, Made by us, then Other rounds folded away.

  • Scroll and the header always tells you which shelf you’re on.
  • Jump chips at the top take you straight to a shelf.
  • Rounds that aren’t today fold into one line each (“Round 3 · Sun · 4 bets”), so they can’t be mistaken for today’s.
Best forPeople who like to scroll everything, without ever losing their place.
COne card, rounds insideKalshi events, with outcomes by date

Each kind of bet appears once, with Round 1 · 2 · 3 as a switch inside the card. It opens on today’s round. A Featured page leads with what’s live, what’s moving and what the group just made.

  • Twelve round cards become four. No more scrolling past the same question.
  • Flip a card to Round 1 to see how it settled, or to Round 3 to get in early.
  • Chips across the top, as now, but each one means a different kind of bet.
Best forThe shortest page, for people who want to compare rounds side by side.

My pick: A, round tabs

  • The trip is lived one round at a time, and so is the betting: almost every bet you’ll place is about today.
  • It’s the pattern DraftKings and FanDuel use for a tournament: tabs for the event and its rounds, “futures” (our “The week”) in their own tab.
  • It’s the clearest “you are here”: one lit tab, one plate, never two of the same bet on screen.
  • It can borrow from C later: a small R1 · R2 · R3 switch on a card, if people want to compare.

What stays the same, whichever you pick

  • The cards: chance, Yes and No, who’s on it, beers bet.
  • 🍺 My bets, Make your own bet, Sharpest in the group.
  • The “About” row of names, to see every bet about one person.
  • Placing a bet takes you back to where you were, in that section.

Where things go

  • The week: MVP, Team Competition, Ryder Cup, hole in one, eagle, most shots of the hole.
  • Each round: under par, birdie a par 3, finds the water, three-putts.
  • Off the course: the pool, last one standing, the closing dinner.
  • Ours: everything the group makes. Settled lives with its round, or at the foot.
