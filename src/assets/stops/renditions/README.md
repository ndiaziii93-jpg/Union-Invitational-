# Three tournament covers, nine pictures

Renditions for the band at the head of the You tab, one picture per stop.
Not in the book yet: the commissioner picks a direction first.

* **a-** Tournament day. The first tee dressed for a competition: bunting,
  tee markers, a scoreboard easel, a trophy on a bench.
* **b-** The club badge. A crest a month in gold on pine, built the same way
  every time, with the month's motif at its side: holly, a snowflake, a
  camellia. The blank ribbon carries no writing.
* **c-** The matchbook cartoon. A 1950s club-cartoon golfer, one gag a
  month. Note: in these three the figure stands where the headline falls,
  so this direction needs redrawing with the action pushed right before it
  could be used.

Every one is 1600 × 898 WebP, no lettering anywhere, because the book lays
its own headline over the picture. Full-resolution originals (2736 × 1536)
are in the commissioner's Blueprint Studio library.
