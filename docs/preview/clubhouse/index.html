Two additions, both small

  • A course chip above the hole strip. Your home course and tees are set once and approved by the commissioner, so the strip loads that card’s pars and indexes.
  • A photo before it counts. When the 18th is in, the sheet asks for a photo of the signed card, taken there and then. The card posts to the stop and to your Union handicap, and the photo sits with the round.

Everything else is the same

  • Hole by hole, Stableford worked out as you go, the same hole drawings and the same keypad.
  • “My round” only. The “Score the group” toggle stays for the week in Vegas, where a marker scores the group.
  • Scramble and pairs stops reuse the pairs mode the Boards already know from Belek.
  • Closes on the stop’s last night. The siren crowns the winner on the Season tab and the Register writes it up like a recap.

How it is locked

Every row in the book is stamped with its season. The server refuses any write to a 2026 row unless the master PIN has reopened that season, so a phone with an old cached page cannot change the record by accident. The app reads both seasons and only ever writes to the open one.

What “the same” means here

  • The six tabs and every screen behind them stay as built. The Clubhouse is a third mode of the same app, switched by date, not a second app.
  • Scoring the group, the marker’s card, the hole strip, the keypad: untouched. A year of virtual rounds on the same screen means nobody relearns it on the first tee.
  • The Boards, the week’s honours, the chat, the recaps and the social pack all run as they did in Belek.
  • Two additions the week gains from the season: the women’s 18-hole division and the partners’ 9 are two more boards, not a new format.
  • The tab bar gains the one word that changes, Vegas for Resort, and the dateline reads the Vegas clock.

Nothing from Türkiye is lost.

The two rules the build keeps

  • No migration. The 2026 rows stay exactly as they are; new seasons add rows beside them under their own prefix.
  • The server guards the past. A rule in the database refuses writes to a closed season. Reopening is a master-PIN action, logged in Manage.
