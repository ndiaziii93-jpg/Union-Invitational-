The book, for the refs

You keep the score for your group. Every stroke goes in on your phone, a hole at a time, and the boards update for everyone the moment you save. It takes a few taps a hole.

Put the book on your phone

Once, before you fly

iPhone

  1. Open the link in Safari.
  2. Tap Share ⇪ at the bottom of the screen.
  3. Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen, then Add.
  4. Open it from the crest on your home screen from now on.

Android

  1. Open the link in Chrome.
  2. Tap the menu ⋮ at the top right.
  3. Tap Install app (or Add to Home screen).
  4. Open it from the crest on your home screen from now on.

Why bother

Installed, the book opens full screen like any app, keeps working with no signal on the course, and updates itself when there is a new version. The first time, open it once on Wi-Fi.

Scoring a round, step by step

Tap a clip to pause it
1Scores · Score the group

Sign in once, and pick your group

  • Tap Scores, stay on Score the group, then Enter PIN.
  • Type your ref PIN and tap Sign in. The phone keeps you signed in for the trip.
  • Tap the line under Score Entry to check the round and pick your group, then Done.

Your PIN comes from the commissioner. Never post it in the group chat, and never hand your phone over signed in.

2Scores · each hole

Score a hole, then save it

  • Tap the first player’s box. A keypad opens: tap their strokes. The par key is outlined; pick up is the most allowed.
  • It jumps to the next player by itself. Red unsaved means it hasn’t gone anywhere yet.
  • Pick a name for Bingo (first on the green), Bango (closest once all are on) and Bongo (first in the hole).
  • Tap Save hole. It goes to the boards and you move on to the next hole.

Not sure? Leave a box empty and ask the group. Nothing counts until you tap Save hole.

3Scores · the keypad

Mulligans and marks

  • A mulligan: open the player’s keypad and tap Front mulligan or Back mulligan. One each per nine; once used, it’s gone.
  • Marks (bottom right) is for the recap: three-putts, water, out of bounds. Optional, and never changes a score.

No mulligans in the Championship Final on Sunday, apart from the breakfast ball on the 1st.

4Scores · the prize holes

Longest drive and closest to the pin

  • On the day’s longest-drive hole (a par 5) and closest-to-the-pin hole (a par 3), a Pin & drive box appears under the hole.
  • Pick whoever is in front. If someone beats it later, pick the new name.

Closest to the pin has no distance to type: the flag on the green is the mark. Whoever beats it moves the flag.

5Scores · any saved hole

Fix a wrong score

  • Tap the hole number along the top.
  • Tap the player, tap the right score, then Save hole again.

Every change shows on the boards straight away, for everyone.

6Scores · the 18th

Finish the group’s round

  • Score and save the 18th like any hole.
  • The book asks Finish Round? Check the card with the players, then tap Finish round.
  • The group’s card is now closed. When the last group finishes, the round closes itself.

Finished too soon? Reopen this group sits at the bottom of the card. Once the whole round has closed, only the master reviewer can reopen it.

7Scores · before the first tee

If the round isn’t open yet

  • Usually the commissioner opens each round. If you see not open for scoring, tap Nominate them.
  • Pick the closest-to-the-pin hole (par 3s only) and the longest-drive hole (par 5s only).
  • Tap Open round and confirm with your PIN.

Good to know

Two refs, one round

Each ref scores only their own group. The two of you can save holes at the same time without getting in each other’s way.

No signal

Keep scoring. Saved holes are kept on your phone and sent the moment there is signal. Don’t close the app with a hole unsaved.

The book reminds you

At the turn it shows how your group stands; on the prize holes it says so; if a Bingo Bango Bongo box is empty after a save, it asks.

Golfers’ own marks

Golfers can mark their own holes in My round. That never touches your card: the score is yours alone.

Photos

The gold camera on the hole picture works for you too: tap it, take a photo or pick one. It’s tagged with the hole.

If something looks wrong

Fix it yourself as in step 5. If the round has already closed, ask the master reviewer.
