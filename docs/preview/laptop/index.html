What changes, and what I would build it with.

Each enhancement names the tool in this workspace that does it. None of them touch the phone or the week in Vegas.

In the rendition

  • A rail instead of the top tabs. You, Season, Vegas HQ, then the Belek book and Manage, with your name at the foot. The nameplate and dateline go; the title row says where you are and what to do.
  • One question a screen, answered by the biggest thing. The button for the most frequent job is in the title row: enter the card, export the headcount, approve a course.
  • Charts on the season. The Order of Merit as a race across the stops, the handicap as a trend with Belek as the first point, a sparkline a golfer in the handicaps table. Palette validated for colour-blind readers; a table under every chart.
  • The field as a table. Name, playing, status, package, the meter, what is left, flights in or not. Totals computed from the rows. Sort by what is outstanding.
  • Keyboard on a virtual round. The number keys score the hole, the arrows move along the strip, Enter posts. The same keypad stays for the mouse.
  • The commissioner's desk. Manage opens as a page here, not a window: packages, money, rooms and courses side by side, the exports one click.

From the other projects' kit

  • Design rules for app screens (the taste skill): the shell, colour only on data, flat surfaces, numbers right-aligned in tabular figures, an inspector that measures the result at 1440, 768 and 390.
  • Data visualisation rules (the dataviz skill): the form by the data's job, a validator that checks the palette, 2px lines, 8px markers, a legend for two series or more, hover and a table view.
  • Course scorecards by lookup (Firecrawl): type a course name and the par and stroke index come back prefilled from the club's own card, for you to check and approve.
  • A face for Vegas (Runway or the asset generator): one photograph of the desert at dusk for the HQ, under the rule that a photo carries a section once, and a monogram for the rail drawn from the crest.
  • The stop's result as a post (Orshot): when a stop crowns its winner, a square and a story card rendered from a template, ready for the group chat and the social pack.
  • The 2026 sheet, read in (Google Drive): the Financials Tracker's per-person charges into the archive, so the money record lives in the book and not the spreadsheet.
