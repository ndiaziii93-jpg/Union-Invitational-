Question one

Changing how you’re joining

The book already knows two of the three things you need. Division says men’s, women’s or not golfing. Package carries the nights and whether it includes golf. What it has no word for is the middle: somebody who is golfing, but not all of it. That is the real gap, and each option below closes it differently.

A · “How you’re joining” — one card, three questionssuggested

Three chip rows in a single card. Lives on the You tab and in Vegas HQ — the same card, both places, one source of truth.

For the tee sheets. Tap the ones you’re playing.

How long you’re there

The commissioner is told. You were on 7 nights · golfer. This is 4 nights, a practice day and two rounds, so the price changes — it shows up on his desk as a change to confirm.

For itSays exactly what the tee sheets and the hotel need. One card, one place, and it reads as a question rather than a form. Handles every Turkey case you named. Against itThree new fields on the sign-up, and the round list has to exist before “which rounds” means anything.

B · Let the package say it

No new model at all. You add more packages; everyone just picks the one that fits.

Your package · change any time

…and one more for each combination somebody asks for.

For itNothing new to build. The money already follows the package, so a change reprices itself and the meters stay honest. Against itThe list multiplies: nights × golf × which rounds. And it still cannot say which two rounds somebody is playing, which is the thing a tee sheet needs.

If you want my answer

Build C, and steal the gold button from A. It stands out because it is doing something none of the cream cards do — showing you the round — rather than because it has been painted a louder colour, and painting things louder is a trick that only works until the second thing gets painted. D is the one to add later if you want it to feel like a golf app rather than a ledger; it is a bolt-on to C, not a different road.
