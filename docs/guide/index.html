    This icon on your home screen. Open it and the crest comes up on pine, then Today loads with the countdown to the first tee.
  3. 3

    Five tabs along the bottom

    TodayScoresBoardsCalendarResort
    Today
    What is on today, the countdown to the next thing, the four games at a glance, and the day's report once it is written.
    Scores
    Where the refs score the group, and where you keep My round, your own notes on your own holes. Tap the hole picture for the big hole with front, middle and back yardages.
    Boards
    Team Competition, MVP, Bingo Bango Bongo, the Ryder Cup, Drives & Pins. They fill themselves as the refs save holes.
    Calendar
    The whole week, dinner to tee time. Tap a day for all of it.
    Resort
    What is open now at the Titanic, where to eat, the spa, the grounds, and a box for anything the book has wrong.
    The crest
    Top left on every page. Tap it and ask the book anything: who is leading, the format tomorrow, who still has the breakfast ball.
    Watch: the hole, up closeThe hand shows every tap. Tap the picture of the hole on Scores. Front, middle and back to the green, a tap anywhere for the distance to that spot, yards or metres, and the arrows walk the holes.
  4. 4

    Only the refs' scores count

    Justin and Manny enter every score for their group on Score the group. If you tap Scores and see "Scoring is closed to you", that is right. It is not a fault, and there is nothing to fix. Keep your own card in whatever app you like; the book takes the refs' card as the card.

  5. 5

    Say how the hole felt

    The one thing to do all week. The refs write what happened; only you know what it felt like, and that is what the day's report is made of.

    On Scores, open My round and pick your name once. Then, when a ref saves a hole worth talking about, a strip appears at the foot of your screen asking How'd it feel? One tap on one of these, a word if you want one, Done.

    RobbedGot away with itWrong clubBottled it

    It changes no score and no board. It is yours, and the holes the whole field agrees robbed them become the story at dinner.

  6. 6

    If something goes wrong

    There is no "Add to Home Screen" option

    You are not in Safari. A link tapped inside WhatsApp opens in a browser that cannot install apps. Copy the link, open Safari yourself, paste it, and try again.

    It looks out of date

    Swipe the app fully closed and open it again from the icon. It fetches the newest build on its own, within a few minutes of one going out. The build number is at the foot of every page.

    It says "Scoring is closed to you"

    Correct, unless you are Justin or Manny. Only the refs score. Everything else is yours to read and My round is yours to write.

    I am a ref and my PIN does not work

    Sign in from the dial at the top right, not from the Scores page. Digits only, no spaces. If it still fails, find Berto. He can set you a new one in a minute, and the old one stops working everywhere the moment he does.

    My name is not on the list

    Tell Berto. The roster is set once and the book redraws on every phone the moment it changes.

    Can I use it with no signal?

    Yes. Once installed it opens and reads with nothing. A ref's saved holes wait on the phone and go up the moment the signal is back. The foot of the page says "Shared" when it is.

PINs, names and the roster: Berto, in person. PINs never go in the group chat.

Anything about a score during a round: your ref, on the course.
