The mascot, three ways

Three ways he could live in the book — on You, on Season, on Vegas HQ, and on the card you fill in. The covers and the layout of You and Vegas HQ do not change in any of them. Everything below moves; watch for a few seconds.

First, a fix that is already in. The drawing shipped with the transparency checkerboard painted into it as real pixels, which is the grey box you saw round him on the iPad. I told you that was just the crop. It was not — it was in the file, and on the live app. It is cut out properly now, and that is what you see here.

AThe caddie who stays

He is always there, standing in the bottom corner above the tabs, dressed for whichever tournament is open. He breathes, and every so often he leans. Tap through the tabs and he is simply there, the way a caddie is there.

For: one character, everywhere, no artwork to redraw. Against: he sits on top of the page rather than belonging to it, and he is in the way of the bottom-right of every screen.

BIn the band

He belongs to the artwork, not to the page: he stands inside the green band at the head of the tab, half in the picture, and slides in as the tab opens. Nothing else moves, and the rest of the screen is left alone entirely.

For: looks designed rather than stuck on; costs no room anywhere else. Against: he is only at the top, so he is gone the moment you scroll, and a tab with no band — the Season list — needs one adding.

CThe one who turns up when there is something to say

He is not on the screen all the time. He leans in from the edge when there is something worth saying — and on the card he reacts to what you just wrote: a jump for a birdie, a wince for a blob.

For: the most character by a distance, and he never gets in the way, because he is only there when he has earned it. Against: the most to build, and a mascot that comes and goes can irritate if he has nothing good to say.

Mixing is allowed. B on the tabs and C on the card is the obvious pair: he belongs to the artwork where there is artwork, and he comes alive where you are actually doing something. Say which and I will build it.

Four suits of clothes exist — santa hat for the Merry Mulligan, bobble hat and cold breath for the Frostbite, rose for the Sweetheart, hat and boots for Vegas. Whichever draft wins, he wears the tournament he is standing in.
