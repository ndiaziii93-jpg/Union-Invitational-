The week, in review

Executive summary

Eleven golfers, thirty-three cards and a field that averaged 94 gross and a shade over two net above par — which, for a week of this much hospitality, is the handicaps doing their job. The story of the scoring is a simple one: everybody started slowly and everybody got eaten by the par 5s. Twenty-three of the thirty-three cards were worse on the front than the back, by two points on average, and the long holes were played at 1.3 over par with twice as many sevens-or-worse as the par 4s.

The individual trend was DP's: 91, 88, 85, the only golfer to improve twice, and Sunday's 85 the low round of the week by three. Duncan's 107 on the same day was the other end of the same pairing, and the reason their team card still read 43 points is that four-ball forgives. Devin took the MVP on net consistency rather than fireworks — three rounds inside four shots of each other — and the UK won the cup on the singles.

The number that should bother everyone: 14 balls out of bounds and 9 pick-ups. That's 23 holes with no score to speak of, across a field that was otherwise competent.

Player report · 1 of 11

DP

Handicap 16 · playing 14 · paired with Duncan W

You were the week's improver and you don't need telling where the shots went. Sunday's 85 split 46 and 39, and the 39 was proper golf: seven pars in nine holes, nothing worse than a six, 21 points on the back nine when your partner was going out of bounds. The front was the same swing with a different head on it — out of bounds off the first tee, two doubles, and a six on every par 5 you looked at.

That last bit is the whole report. You played six par 5s on Sunday and made six, six, six, six, six and seven. Not a par among them, and not one lost to the tee shot — you're hitting the second from 220 and making the number that second shot invites. Lay up to a full wedge on every one and you take four shots off a round without changing a thing about your swing.

The putter cost you four more: 33 putts with three-putts on 5, 7, 16 and 18, all from distance. Your lag putting, not your stroke. Get the first one inside three feet and the week's 85 is an 81, and the 81 plays to about eleven.

Three things: a tee-shot routine on the first, lay up on every par 5, lag putts to a three-foot circle. Do two of them and you're off 13 by the spring.
