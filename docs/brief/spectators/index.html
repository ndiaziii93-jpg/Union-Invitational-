Put the book on your phone

Once, before you fly

iPhone

  1. Open the link in Safari.
  2. Tap Share ⇪ at the bottom of the screen.
  3. Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen, then Add.
  4. Open it from the crest on your home screen from now on.

Android

  1. Open the link in Chrome.
  2. Tap the menu ⋮ at the top right.
  3. Tap Install app (or Add to Home screen).
  4. Open it from the crest on your home screen from now on.

Why bother

Installed, the book opens full screen like any app, keeps working with no signal on the course, and updates itself when there is a new version. The first time, open it once on Wi-Fi.

Finding your way round

Tap a clip to pause it
Today

The front page

  • Today shows the day’s round, the first tee and what is on next, with a countdown.
  • Below that, the live standings from every board. Tap a card to open that board.
  • Then the day’s plan, hour by hour.

Once a round is over, The Day’s Recap appears here: a short write-up of how the day went.

Boards

Follow the scores, live

  • Tap Boards. The first page sums up every competition.
  • Along the top: Team (the pairs), MVP (the best individual), Bingo, the Cup (UK against USA) and Prizes.
  • Each table shows how many holes each player is through, and updates as holes are saved.

Most boards have a Full rules link that explains how they are scored.

Boards · Photos

Photos from the course

  • Under Boards → Photos, the golfers’ and refs’ photos appear as they are taken, sorted by round.
  • Each one says which hole it is from. Tap one to see it full size.
Calendar

The week

  • Calendar starts with today, then every day of the trip.
  • Tap a day for everything on it: tee times, dinners, the ceremonies.
Resort

The Titanic

  • Pick what you are after, Hungry, A drink, Late bite, Pools, Spa, Help, and see what is open right now.
  • Every place has a card: the hotel’s photo, open or shut, included or extra, today’s hours and where it is. Swipe it down, or tap above it, to close.
  • Search finds anything: try “heated”, “Irish” or “steak”. Further down, the aerial photo shows each part of the grounds; tap one to see what is there.

The package includes one à la carte dinner per seven-night stay, at Pascarella. It needs booking at reception, so book early. Guests on the five-day trip should ask reception whether theirs is included.

Good to know

It updates itself

Scores and photos arrive on their own while the book is open. There is nothing to refresh.

You can’t break anything

Only the refs and the master reviewer can enter or change a score, and they need a PIN to do it.

No signal

Once it has been opened, the book keeps working offline and catches up when the signal is back.

Hotel hours

The resort has confirmed it is keeping its summer hours for our week, and the book shows those. If a time is wrong on the ground, tell a ref.
