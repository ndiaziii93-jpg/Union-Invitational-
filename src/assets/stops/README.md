# The stop covers

One painting a stop, for the band of photograph at the head of the You tab
while that stop is open. 1600 × 898, WebP, drawn to a shared recipe so the
eleven of them read as one set:

* a 1930s travel-poster treatment, flat matte shapes, paper grain
* the book's own palette — pine, cream, antique gold, sage — and one accent
  colour a month: lantern gold in December, pale ice blue in January, deep
  camellia red in February
* the horizon across the vertical middle, so the same picture survives the
  phone's 2:1 crop and the laptop's 4.4:1 band
* the left third and the lower edge kept calm, because the kicker, the
  headline and the day's one button are laid over them
* no words, no letters, no numbers, no signage: the headline is the book's

Made with the commissioner's Blueprint Studio account; the full-resolution
originals (2736 × 1536 PNG) are in that library.
