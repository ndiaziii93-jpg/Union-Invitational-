Put the book on your phone

Once, before you fly

iPhone

  1. Open the link in Safari.
  2. Tap Share ⇪ at the bottom of the screen.
  3. Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen, then Add.
  4. Open it from the crest on your home screen from now on.

Android

  1. Open the link in Chrome.
  2. Tap the menu ⋮ at the top right.
  3. Tap Install app (or Add to Home screen).
  4. Open it from the crest on your home screen from now on.

Why bother

Installed, the book opens full screen like any app, keeps working with no signal on the course, and updates itself when there is a new version. The first time, open it once on Wi-Fi.

Your round, step by step

Tap a clip to pause it
1Scores · My round

Tell the book who you are

  • Tap Scores on the bar at the bottom, then My round.
  • Tap your name. The phone remembers you from then on.
  • Tap any number along the top to jump to a hole.

Picked the wrong name? Tap Not you? beside your name and choose again.

2Scores · My round

Mark a hole worth remembering

  • Tap Went right or Went wrong, then the one that fits: Got away with it, Pured it, Clutch, or the other three.
  • Add a few words in the box if you like.
  • Tap Next hole to move on.

All optional. The marks and notes feed the day’s recap. They never change a score.

3Scores · the hole

Send a photo from the course

  • Tap the gold camera on the picture of the hole.
  • Your phone asks: Take Photo or Photo Library.
  • The photo is tagged with the hole and shows up for everyone under Boards → Photos.

No signal on the course? The photo waits on your phone and sends itself when there is signal.

4Boards

See where you stand

  • Tap Boards. The first page sums up every competition.
  • Tap a name along the top for the full table: Team, MVP, Bingo, Cup, Prizes.
  • Most boards have a Full rules link if you want to know how they are worked out.

The boards update as each hole is saved, so they are live during the round.

5Today

Your day, at a glance

  • Today shows the day’s round, the first tee time and what is on next.
  • Below it: the live standings and the day’s plan, hour by hour.
6Resort

Off the course: the Titanic

  • Pick what you are after, Hungry, A drink, Late bite, Pools, Spa, Help, and see what is open now.
  • Every place has a card: photo, open or shut, included or extra, hours, where it is. Swipe it down to close.
  • Search finds anything. The aerial photo shows each part of the grounds.

The package includes one à la carte dinner per seven-night stay, at Pascarella. Book it at reception early in the week. On the five-day trip? Ask reception whether yours is included.

Good to know

You never type a stroke

The refs enter every score for the group. If a number on the boards looks wrong, tell your ref or the master reviewer; they can put it right.

A quick question after a big hole

After a birdie, a double or a nominated hole, a strip may ask how it felt. One tap answers it, or skip it. It asks once.

No signal

The book works offline once it has been opened. Marks and photos wait on your phone and go up by themselves.

PINs are for the refs

Only the refs and the master reviewer have a PIN. Never post one in the group chat.
