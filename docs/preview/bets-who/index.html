Betting on people

How “who” bets and tagging would look in the Bets tab, before it is built. Three phones, all live: tap a name, a person, a stake. Everything is in beers, a quarter at a time, as now.

1The Bets listWho cards and a filter by person
  • Each “anyone” question becomes one card: Anyone first, then each golfer with his own chance. It opens out to all eleven.
  • New ones from the refs’ marks: who finds the water, who three-putts. They settle themselves.
  • The row of names at the top filters the list: tap Ray V to see every bet about Ray.
  • Your own bets show the people tagged on them.
2One who bet, openedPick a golfer, put a stake on him
  • The names along the top: tap one to bet on him. Each is its own yes/no, like MVP now.
  • Long shots pay: a ¼ 🍺 on Robby B at 1% pays about 25 🍺 if he holes one.
  • It settles the moment a ref saves a 1 on his card. Everyone else in that card settles No when Round 3 is locked.
3Make your own, with peopleTag who it’s about, or ask who
  • Yes or no: tap the people it’s about. They’re tagged on the card and get a buzz.
  • Who will it be?: pick the people as the answers, like “Who’s last to breakfast?”. Each gets an even chance to start.
  • Tap Open the bet to see the card it makes and the notification the tagged person gets.

What settles itself

  • Hole in one and eagle, this week: from the scores, for anyone and for each golfer.
  • Each round: who birdies a par 3, who finishes under par net (scores); who finds the water, who three-putts (the refs’ marks).
  • Most shots of the hole across the week: from the refs’ marks, once Round 3 is locked.
  • Your own bets, and who-bets you make, are settled by you or the commissioner, as now; a guest’s by the commissioner.

Kept out on purpose

  • Bets on one golfer’s score on one hole: 11 golfers × 18 holes × 3 rounds is a list nobody can read.
  • Marks a ref forgot to make can’t pay out: a water bet is only as good as the marking, so the rules on each one say so.
